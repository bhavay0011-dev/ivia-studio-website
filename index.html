Questions people ask first
Anything else, ask us on WhatsApp. A human answers that one.

    

      
Do I need to buy hosting or a domain myself?
No. We register the domain in your name, set up hosting, SSL and business email, and hand you the logins. You own everything.

      
Can you take over or redo my existing website?
Yes. We audit what you have, keep what works (content, SEO, domain history) and rebuild the rest, with redirects so you do not lose Google traffic.

      
Will my website be SEO-friendly?
Yes. We build SEO-friendly websites as standard: fast loading, mobile-first, clean headings and code, page titles and descriptions, schema markup, a sitemap, and Google Search Console set up at launch.

      
How long does a website take?
It depends on scope. A business site is weeks, not months; a custom app takes longer. You get a dated plan after the first call, and weekly previews after that.

      
Can you guarantee the #1 spot on Google?
Nobody honest can guarantee organic or map rankings, and Google warns against anyone who does. What we do is the work that moves rankings, and if you want the top spot right away, Google Ads can put you there.

      
Is the WhatsApp bot official?
Yes. We use the official WhatsApp Business Platform through Meta, so your number is verified for business use and your messages follow WhatsApp's policies.
