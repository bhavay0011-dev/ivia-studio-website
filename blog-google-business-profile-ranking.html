How to rank higher on Google Maps with your Google Business Profile

When someone searches "dentist near me" or "bakery in Pune", the map results at the top get most of the clicks. Those results come from Google Business Profiles. Here is how Google decides who shows up, and what you can actually change.

The three things Google says it looks at

Google states that local results are based mainly on relevance, distance and prominence.

  • Relevance: how well your profile matches the search. This is where categories, services and descriptions matter.
  • Distance: how far you are from the searcher or the place in the search. You cannot change this, so do not fake an address.
  • Prominence: how well known you are, online and off. Reviews, links, mentions and your website all feed in.

Get the profile right

  • Primary category: the single most important choice. Pick the most specific one that is true, such as "Pediatric dentist" rather than "Dentist" if that is what you are.
  • Secondary categories for other real services.
  • Services and products listed in full, with plain descriptions.
  • Accurate hours, including holidays. Being shown as closed costs visits.
  • Name exactly as your signboard. Adding keywords to your business name breaks Google's guidelines and can get the profile suspended.

Reviews: steady beats sudden

  • Ask every happy customer. A short link or QR code at the counter helps.
  • Reply to every review, good and bad, politely and specifically.
  • Never buy reviews or offer rewards for them. Google removes them and can penalise the profile.

Photos and posts

  • Upload real photos regularly: storefront, interior, team, work and products.
  • Use posts for offers, events and updates. They keep the profile active and give searchers a reason to choose you.

Your website still matters

Google connects your profile to your website. A fast, SEO-friendly site with a clear page for each service and each area you serve, the same name, address and phone as your profile, and LocalBusiness schema makes your profile stronger.

Quick wins this week: check your primary category, fill in every service, fix your hours, add ten real photos, and reply to every unanswered review.

When to add Google Ads

Map rankings build over weeks and months. If you need calls now, Local Services Ads or Google Ads can put you at the top while the organic work catches up.

Key points

  • Google ranks local results by relevance, distance and prominence.
  • Your primary category is the most important setting on your profile.
  • Collect reviews steadily, reply to all of them and never buy them.
  • A fast website with matching details and schema strengthens your profile.
