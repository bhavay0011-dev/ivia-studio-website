WhatsApp Business App vs API: which one does your business need?

Many businesses start on the free WhatsApp Business App and hit a wall: too many chats, no automation, one phone. The WhatsApp Business Platform, usually called the API, solves that, but it works differently. Here is what changes.

The WhatsApp Business App

A free app from Meta, built for small businesses.

  • Business profile, catalogue, labels, quick replies and simple greeting and away messages.
  • Runs on a phone, with a limited number of linked devices.
  • No real chatbot, no integration with your website or CRM, and broadcasts only reach people who saved your number.

Good for: a shop, clinic or freelancer handling a manageable number of chats personally.

The WhatsApp Business Platform (API)

The API has no app of its own. You connect your number through Meta's Cloud API or an official provider, then use a dashboard, a chatbot or your own software on top.

  • Chatbots and automation: answer FAQs, book appointments, take orders, send payment links and route to a human when needed.
  • Many agents on one number, with chats assigned and tracked.
  • Integrations: order updates from your store, reminders from your booking system, leads into your CRM.
  • Template messages to customers who opted in, such as order confirmations, reminders and offers.

Rules that come with the API

  • Opt-in is required. You can only message people who agreed to hear from you.
  • The 24-hour window. When a customer messages you, you can reply freely for 24 hours. Outside that window, you must use a pre-approved template.
  • Templates are reviewed by Meta before you can use them.
  • Meta charges for some messages, mainly business-initiated templates. Rates vary by country and category, so check Meta's current pricing page.
  • Quality matters. If many people block or report you, your sending limits drop.
Important: a number registered on the API cannot be used in the regular WhatsApp app at the same time. Plan the move, or use a new number for the API.

When to move to the API

  • You miss enquiries because nobody can reply fast enough.
  • More than one person needs to answer chats.
  • Customers keep asking the same questions.
  • You want order, booking or payment updates sent automatically.

Avoid unofficial tools

Bulk-sender tools and browser extensions that automate the normal app break WhatsApp's terms. Numbers using them are frequently banned. We only build on the official WhatsApp Business Platform.

Key points

  • The free app suits one person handling a manageable number of chats.
  • The API adds chatbots, multiple agents, integrations and template messages.
  • The API needs opt-in, follows a 24-hour reply window and uses Meta-approved templates.
  • Avoid unofficial bulk senders; they get numbers banned.
